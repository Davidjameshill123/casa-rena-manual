🏡 Welcome to Casa Rena!

Sunshine? Check. Sangria? Double check. Everything else you need to know? Right here—simple, breezy, and just the right amount of bossy.

🔑 Getting In

  • Look at the inside left edge of the side gate—there’s a slim plastic housing.
  • Flick off the cap, grab the key, unlock the gate, then put it back and reseal the cap.
  • Go up the side stairs to the roof terrace.
  • Find the wooden four-poster bed frame.
  • The lockbox is on the back side of the rear upright post, closest to the back wall.
  • Lockbox code: 1684
  • Inside you’ll find: Unmarked front door key and Safe key (attached)

🚨 Alarm Instructions

  • Pull the front door towards you while turning the key anti-clockwise.
  • The alarm will start beeping.
  • Reach above the stove extractor fan, grab the fob.
  • Tap it to the alarm panel by the door to disable.
  • Return fob and key to their hiding places.

🧷 Keys (by the TV):

  • Electric gate fob
  • Security fob
  • Side gate key (peach)
  • Front gate key (pink)
  • Pool cupboard key (blue)

🏠 While You're In

  • Kitchen bi-fold: open the single door first, then fold the rest.
  • Rear sliding doors: unlock and unscrew bolt — do not exit from the rear.
  • Bedroom windows: can stay open when you leave (they're security-barred).

🍷 Wine Fridge

  • On/off switch is on the inside top panel.
  • You may use the wine/beer/food — please replace what you use.

👋 When You’re Leaving

  • Press the house-with-person icon to arm the alarm.
  • Close the door — it auto-locks.
  • Pull the door shut and lock it for extra safety.

🧼 Departure Checklist

  • Clean the toaster (crumbs!)
  • Empty and clean dishwasher
  • Remove metal items immediately after washing
  • Use eco-speedy wash — symbol: running plate + eco light
  • Wipe fridge shelves, throw out expired food
  • Label jars when opening
  • No bread left behind
  • Throw out old dishcloths
  • Empty bins and take them to communal bins every 2 days
  • Bleach the sinks + toilets
  • Wipe down all surfaces + use insect floor wash
  • Laundry: wash if time, otherwise leave dry in basket
  • Leave washer door open, wipe seal, clean drawer

⚡ Electricity Tips

  • 🛏 Off Peak: Midnight to 8am (weekdays + weekends)
  • 😌 Mid Peak: 8am - 10am, 2pm - 6pm, 10pm - Midnight
  • 🔥 Peak: 10am - 2pm, 6pm - 10pm
  • Cold washes can run anytime
  • For hot washes, use delay timer for off-peak
  • Same for dishwasher
