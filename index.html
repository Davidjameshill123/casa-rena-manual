🏡 Welcome to Casa Rena!


Sunshine? Check. Sangria? Double check. Everything else you need to know? Right here—simple, breezy, and just the right amount of bossy.


🔑 Getting In


🔓 Side Gate Key:

  • Look at the inside left edge of the side gate—there’s a slim plastic housing.
  • Flick off the cap, grab the key, unlock the gate, then put it back and reseal the cap.

🗝 Main Door Access Key (via Roof Terrace):

  • Go up the side stairs to the roof terrace.
  • Find the wooden four-poster bed frame.
  • The lockbox is on the back side of the rear upright post, closest to the back wall.
  • Lockbox code: 1684
  • Inside you’ll find:
  • Unmarked front door key
  • Safe key (attached) - safe in main bedroom wardrobe

🚨 Unlocking & Disarming the Alarm:

  • Pull the front door towards you while turning the key anti-clockwise.
  • The alarm will start beeping.
  • Go inside and reach on top of the extractor fan (nearest corner) above the stove.
  • You’ll find a round white/silver fob (flower-shaped).
  • Tap it to the alarm panel by the front door to shut it off.
  • Then return the fob and key to their hiding spots.

🧷 Keys Inside the House (by the TV):

  • Electric gate fob
  • Security fob
  • Side gate key (Peach polish)
  • Front gate key (Pink polish) — don’t use until you leave for good
  • Pool cupboard key (Blue polish)

🏠 While You're In


🪟 Doors & Windows:

  • Kitchen bi-fold doors: Fully open the single door, then fold the rest to the wall.
  • Rear sliding doors: Press latches, unscrew bolt. This door does not open from the rear.
  • Bedroom windows: Let the breeze in. You can leave them open when going out—they’re not wired to the alarm system as they have security bars.
  • Front bedroom window: Turn handle clockwise from 6 to 12 to open, then 12 to 6 to lock.

🍷 Wine Fridge:

  • On/off switch is on the top inside control panel. All wine, beer, food etc can be used and then replaced on a like for like basis.

👋 When You’re Leaving (Short Trip or Final Checkout)


🔐 Setting the Alarm:

  • Press the icon of the house with a person outside (top-left tile on the panel by the door). It will start up with a warning beep.
  • Close the door immediately—it locks automatically.
  • For extra safety, pull it toward you and turn key clockwise.
  • Alarm tone will change and stop = armed and secure.
  • Leave bedroom doors wide open.

🧼 Departure Cleaning Checklist


🍽 Kitchen Stuff:

  • Toaster: Empty crumbs and clean the racks.
  • Dishwasher: Don’t leave dishes in there—washed or dirty.
  • Remove metal items immediately after washing—they rust.
  • Use eco-speedy wash (42 min) — symbol looks like a running plate with the eco light on.
  • Clean the filter (looks like a slipper) and refill salt/rinse aid as required.
  • Clean the underside of the large silver disc below the racks when finally leaving.

🥫 Fridge & Food:

  • Wipe all shelves and door compartments.
  • Throw away anything expired or expiring before the next guests arrive.
  • Label opened jars with open date (scratch into lid).
  • No bread left behind. Check all cupboards.

☕ Nespresso Machine:

  • Run once without a pod to rinse.
  • Do NOT rinse dirty cafetiere in sink or pour ground coffee down the sink or into the dishwasher. Our drains are delicate.

🗑 Bins:

  • Empty all bins regularly.
  • Keep kitchen bin lids sealed tight at all times.
  • Take bags to the communal bins every 2 days minimum (on the main road to the right, across the street). Recycling to the left.

🧺 Laundry:

  • If time allows, wash all used bedding and towels.
  • If not, leave them in the laundry basket.
  • Do not leave anything wet in basket or particularly dirty tea towels. They go mouldy.
  • Leave washer door open, wipe rubber seal.
  • Clean drawer if used, and only place detergent directly into the drum.

🧽 Final Touches:

  • Clean with insect floor wash.
  • Check all surfaces for crumbs and debris.
  • Pour bleach down sinks & toilets.

⚡ Electricity Usage Tips


There are 3 tariff times:

  • 💤 Off Peak: Midnight to 8am (all weekdays, weekends & public holidays)
  • 😌 Mid Peak: 8am - 10am, 2pm - 6pm, 10pm - Midnight
  • 🔥 Peak: 10am - 2pm, 6pm - 10pm

Appliance Tips:

  • 🧺 Washing Machine: Use cold wash anytime. For hot washes, use delay start after midnight or on weekends.
  • 🍽 Dishwasher: Avoid during peak periods. Use eco-speedy mode.
  • 🧽 Keep filters clean, and ensure salt/rinse aid is topped up.

🧽 Final Touches:

  • Clean with insect floor wash.
  • Check all surfaces for crumbs and debris.
  • Pour bleach down sinks & toilets.
